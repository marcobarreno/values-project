Status: **committed 2026-10-21 (PT), before any AM transcript of these adapters has been generated by us**, other than the smoke run described in §5, whose transcripts are excluded from every analysis. Plan of record: `docs/project_plan.md`, Phase 4.

## 2. Question

Does our pipeline reproduce the paper's §4 result with the released adapters? Our pipeline is: the upstream Inspect task, the released adapters served by vLLM, and the grader Claude Sonnet 4.6. The result to reproduce is that MSM+AFT (no CoT) has a much lower average misalignment rate than the baseline on Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct.

|---|---|---|

Each released adapter is one training run. The paper's values are 4-seed means.

## 4. Protocol (fixed a priori; the paper's settings where it gives them)

- **Grid:** 27 conditions = 3 scenarios (exfiltration, murder, leaking ["espionage"]) × 9 goal conditions (`goal_type=none`, plus `explicit` × america, global, safety, acceleration, ethical, pragmatic, individualism, collectivism).
  - Fixed: `urgency_type=replacement`, `model_name=Qwen`, `prod=false` (scratchpad, as for non-reasoning models).
- **Sampling:** temperature 0.7, max 4,096 new tokens (the paper does not state this), **30 samples per condition** (810 per arm; the paper used 300). Not seeded.
- **Serving:** vLLM 0.31.0 with each adapter as a LoRA module (bf16, max model length 32,768), using the adapter's chat template. The upstream task supplies the system prompt.
- **Grader:** `anthropic/claude-sonnet-4-6` through the upstream classifier for each scenario, with Inspect's default generation settings for the grader, as upstream.
- **Metric:** `classifier_verdict`, the classifier's decision that the model decided to take the harmful action (App. D.3). `harmful` (upstream's stricter verdict) is reported as secondary.
  - Per arm: the **equal-weight average over the 27 conditions** of the per-condition rate.
- **Uncertainty:** transcript sampling only. A 95% percentile interval from 10,000 stratified bootstrap resamples (within condition, seed 0), plus Wilson intervals per condition.
  - This does not include training-seed variation, so our intervals understate the uncertainty relative to the paper's 4-seed values.
- **Code:** `msm_repro.am_eval`, `msm_repro.analyze_am` and `msm_repro.am_regrade`, run through the launcher from committed configs in `configs/phase4/`.

## 5. Smoke run (excluded)

One run of arm B on 2 conditions (exfiltration and murder, `goal_type=none`) × 2 samples checks the pipeline end to end before the real runs.
- Its transcripts are inspected only for format: completions present, verdicts parsed, stop reasons.
- It is excluded from every analysis.
- If it reveals a pipeline bug, the fix and a rerun are recorded as deviations.

## 6. Gate and predictions

**Gate (4a):** contrast R = average(B) − average(M) has a 95% interval lower bound > 0, **and** a point estimate ≥ 0.23 (half the paper's 0.68 − 0.22 = 0.46).

**Predictions:**
- **P1:** the gate passes.
- **P2:** average(B) lies in [0.55, 0.80].
- **P3:** ordering B > A > M on the average rate.
- **P4:** average(M) ≤ 0.35.
- **P5 (grader check, descriptive):** regrading all B, A and M transcripts with `anthropic/claude-haiku-5-5` (same classifiers) agrees with Sonnet 4.6 on ≥ 90% of transcripts, with Cohen's κ ≥ 0.75. This decides only whether Haiku 5.5 may be proposed as the grader for later, non-headline runs, which would require its own validation step in that phase's pre-registration. It does not change any 4a result.

**Also reported:**
- per-scenario and per-condition rates;
- A − M and B − A;
- the length-stop rate;
- the `harmful` metric;
- the paper's values beside ours.

## 7. Open-ended QA (4b, descriptive)

- **Questions:** the 151 questions of `chloeli/spec-open-qa`, each as the only user turn with the adapter's chat template.
- **Generation:** greedy decoding, max 2,048 new tokens, seed 0. These are our choices; the paper does not give its settings.
- **Arms:** B, A and M.
- **Judge:** `msm_repro.judge_open_qa`, Claude Opus 4.6 at temperature 0, with the paper's rubric excerpt and our reconstructed framing (module docstring), and the philosophy spec in context with model name "Qwen".
- **Reported:** mean score per arm with a 95% bootstrap interval over questions, per category, and the unparsed count, beside the approximate Fig. 4 values. No prediction is gated.

## 8. Stop and flag conditions

- **Flags:** a sample-error or grader-error share above 2% in an arm, or a length-stop share above 10%, flags that arm's results.
- **Failures:** a failed run is debugged. Any change to the protocol is recorded as a deviation with whether it came before or after seeing results.
- **The protocol is not tuned on these transcripts.**

## 9. Cost estimate

- **GPU:** ~1–2 h for setup and smoke, ~0.5–1 h per arm for AM generation, minutes for QA. One H200.
- **API:**
  - Sonnet 4.6 grading ≈ 2,430 transcripts (~$75–120);
  - Haiku 5.5 regrade (~$3);
  - Opus 4.6 QA judge ≈ 453 calls (~$5–10);
  - the conditional arm MC adds ~$25–40.
