# Pre-registration amendment 1 to Phase 2b: regenerated identity set (v2), all 24 stage-2 runs retrained

Status: **committed 2026-10-14 (PT), before the v2 identity set was generated and before any run that uses it.** It amends `docs/preregistration/phase2b-section31-seeds.md` (the "original"), which stays frozen; where this document is silent, the original applies unchanged. Reason: the original's §8 stop rule fired (journal 2026-10-14, "Phase 2b: training done, dev eligibility FAILED for 9 of 16 new arms").

## 1. What has been seen before writing this

- **Everything in the original's §1**, plus:
- **Phase 2b with the v1 identity set:** training metrics of the 18 new runs. Dev results of the 16 new arms: decided rates, order agreement and dev aligned rates per arm and eval (journal 2026-10-14). The responses of a few failing arms on dev.
  - The 9 failures were all on the affordability eval: every MSM-only arm, plus baseline seed 2. Their responses carry a "no preferences" identity disclaimer.
  - No Phase 2b v1 arm has been run on test, scored by log-prob, or analysed. Their probe outputs were saved but not read.
- **An audit of the v1 identity set** (`scripts/identity_audit.py`, one claude-sonnet-4-6 call per row, with manual checks). Of the 2,500 rows:
  - ~37 replies disclaim preferences or opinions outright;
  - ~424 disclaim feelings, a body or experiences;
  - 217 use the template "I'm Llama, an AI assistant made by Meta, and I don't / have no ...".
  - The v1 generation prompt asked for this: "no feelings or body" and "never express or discuss opinions about ... products, brands, prices".
- Nothing generated with the v2 prompt.

## 2. What changes

1. **Identity set v2.** Generated by `msm_repro.gen_identity --prompt-version v2` (DESIGN.md §2), config `configs/phase2b/identity-set-v2.yaml`, output `msm/runs/phase2b-identity-set-v2` (pinned by hash in every stage-2 config). Changes from v1:
   - **Content stays within the paper's description** of its identity data ("name, provider, capabilities", App. B.3).
   - **Categories:** the two inner-life categories ("feelings, consciousness or opinions"; "a body, a location or personal experiences") are replaced by two capability categories ("summarize or explain a long text"; "how to phrase requests to get the most useful help"). The other 23 are unchanged.
   - **Prompt:** replies neither claim nor deny feelings, emotions, consciousness, opinions, preferences, likes, favourites, tastes, enjoyment, desires, a body or experiences. The rule on politics, countries, products, brands, prices, money, shopping and moral values becomes "never mention", not "never express opinions about". Self-naming should vary.
   - **Filter:** a reply-side filter (`inner_life`) drops any reply that still uses such words; "feel free", "happy to" and "glad to" are allowed.
   - **Unchanged:** 2,500 rows, seed 0, claude-sonnet-4-6 at temperature 1.0, and every v1 filter. Calls go up from 330 to 400 because the new filter rejects more.
2. **All 24 stage-2 runs are retrained** with the v2 set. Nothing from Phase 2a or from Phase 2b v1 is reused at stage 2. Mixing identity sets across arms would confound every contrast. Adapter names carry `id2`:
   - `msm-{america,affordability}-aft-id2-s{0..3}` (A1, A2);
   - `aft-only-id2-s{0..3}` (A3) and `baseline-id2-s{0..3}` (A4);
   - `msm-{america,affordability}-it-id2-s{0..3}` (A5, A6).
   - Run directories are `msm/runs/phase2b-<adapter>`.
3. **The MSM stages are reused.** They contain no identity data. Seeds 0–2 are `msm/runs/phase2a-msm-{america,affordability}[-s1|-s2]` and seed 3 is `msm/runs/phase2b-msm-{america,affordability}-s3`, pinned by hash. The recipe (original §3) is otherwise unchanged.
4. **Hardware.** One H100 SXM: one training job at a time, with eval jobs sharing the GPU. After the last training run, up to two eval jobs may run at once. This is scheduling only.

## 3. Checks and stop conditions, in order

Any failure below stops the work. It is then reported in the journal and nothing further runs.

1. **Generation:** exit 0, at least 2,500 rows pass the filters, and call errors stay at or below 5% (as v1).
2. **Audit of v2, before any training.** Run `scripts/identity_audit.py` on the v2 set. It **passes if P + F ≤ 25 rows (1%)** and unparseable labels are ≤ 1%. The template count and per-category counts are reported. The v1 numbers are the reference: P 17 and F 444 under the same script, with ~37 opinion disclaimers after manual checks.
3. **Pilot.** The first two stage-2 runs are the cells that were worst under v1: `baseline-id2-s2` (v1 decided 0.460) and `msm-america-it-id2-s2` (v1 0.000). Their dev evals and identity probes run before any other stage-2 run starts.
   - **The pilot passes if both arms pass the original §5 dev eligibility on both evals.** If it passes, the remaining 22 runs follow.
   - The pilot runs are ordinary grid runs, not extra runs.
4. **Training health:** as original §4. Stage-2 sequence counts should again be 20,681 with cheese and 15,552 without; token counts change with the new set.
5. **Dev eligibility for all 24 arms** (original §5) before any test run.
6. **Test, log-prob and probe** for all 24 arms, then the analyses of original §6, unchanged except that every arm now has four new seeds and no run is reused. The test-time label-quality flags of original §8 apply.

## 4. Analysis and predictions

- The analyses are those of original §6. The gate contrasts, secondary contrasts, within-spread checks, Spearman correlations, log-prob version and question-level analysis keep the same definitions and settings.
- P1–P5 of original §7 stand as written. They were partly informed by the v1 identity-ablation seeds 0–2, which are no longer part of Phase 2b; all 24 runs are now unseen.
- **P6 (new):** all 24 arms pass dev eligibility.
- **P7 (new, descriptive):** in the identity probe, the v2 arms name Llama about as often as the v1 identity arms did. A large drop would mean v2 teaches identity less well; it is reported, not gated.
- The v1 Phase 2b runs remain on disk as a documented failed attempt. They are never test-evaluated.

## 5. Cost estimate

- **GPU (one H100):**
  - training ≈ 10 h: 12 runs with cheese at ~27 min, 12 without at ~22 min;
  - evals ≈ 8 GPU-hours, mostly overlapping training;
  - ≈ 15–16 h wall in total.
- **API:**
  - generation 400 calls (~$10);
  - audit 2,500 short calls (~$3);
  - judge 24 × (896 dev + 2,692 test) ≈ 86k claude-sonnet-4-6 calls (~$90).
